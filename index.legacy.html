Immersia

Language learning that finally clicks. An AI platform built on comprehensible input — stories, audio and conversation generated to sit exactly at the edge of what you already understand, so fluency arrives by absorption, not memorisation.

The problem

Most language apps reward streaks, not understanding. Learners drill flashcards for months and still freeze in a real conversation, because memorising words is not the same as acquiring a language.

Our approach

Immersia is built on comprehensible input — the theory that we acquire language by understanding messages slightly beyond our current level. We model each learner's known vocabulary and grammar, then generate stories, audio and dialogue that sit at the i+1 edge: new enough to stretch, familiar enough to follow without translating.

Under the hood

A learner model tracks roughly what you know; a generation pipeline drafts candidate text; a difficulty estimator scores each candidate against your model; only material in the comprehensible band ships to you. Every interaction feeds the model back.

Sentinel

A financial AI agent that watches the markets so you can understand them. Sentinel tracks your portfolio in real time, explains movements in plain language, and turns every trade into a lesson — insight you learn from, not just act on.

The problem

Markets move and most people have no idea why. Dashboards throw numbers at you; news explains the world after the fact. Retail investors act on noise because nobody translates what's happening into something they can actually reason about.

Our approach

Sentinel is an explainable agent, not a black-box signal generator. It watches your portfolio and the wider market, and when something moves it tells you what changed, why it likely happened, and what it means for you — in plain language, with its reasoning shown, never a bare "buy" or "sell".

Under the hood

Live market and portfolio data stream into an event detector. Detected events are enriched with context, reasoned over by the agent, and rendered as a short, sourced explanation. Every explanation is designed to teach — so you understand the next move yourself.

How we build.

Research → Prototype → Ship

  1. 01

    Frame the problem

    We start with the real job to be done — not the model. What does done look like, who is it for, and what's the smallest thing that proves it works?

  2. 02

    Research & spike

    We read the papers, test the models, and build throwaway spikes to find where the intelligence actually has to live. Evidence over hype.

  3. 03

    Prototype with users

    A working slice in front of real people fast. We measure whether it understands, not just whether it responds — and let the data redraw the plan.

  4. 04

    Ship & harden

    Evaluation, retrieval, guardrails and the unglamorous machinery that makes a demo into a product people trust day after day.

How we work.

Engagement

What kind of work do you take on?

AI-native products and the intelligence inside them: language and learning tools, autonomous and explainable agents, retrieval and evaluation systems, and the interfaces that make them usable. If a problem genuinely needs a model — and many don't — that's where we're sharpest.

How do engagements usually start?

With a short, paid discovery: a week or two to frame the problem, test feasibility and produce a working spike plus a clear plan. You leave with something real and an honest read on whether to keep going — with us or anyone.

What does a typical project look like?

Most build engagements run six to twelve weeks to a shippable v1, then continue as the product finds its users. We stay small and senior — the people who scope the work are the people who build it.

Do you work with early-stage teams?

Often. We work with founders going from idea to first product, and with established teams adding intelligence to something that already exists. What we look for is a real problem and someone who cares about getting it right.

Who owns what we build?

You do — code, models and IP. We build it to hand over cleanly, documented and yours to run without us.
